06 — Principles
01

Cool neutrals over warm cream — reads as a calibrated instrument, not one brand's packaging, so it doesn't fight a clinic or an auto-detailer.

02

Teal and amber instead of blue and terracotta — genuinely underused, genuinely survivable once a client's own brand color replaces the accent token.

03

One shadow token, reserved for real overlays. Everywhere else, borders and space do the work — the fastest way to look hand-built instead of templated.
